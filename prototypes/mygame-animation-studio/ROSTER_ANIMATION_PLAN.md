# Further character animation production

The current release supplies fourteen east-view sets with seventeen clips each and an additional Pendle reload. Each character has 112 base/combat/traversal drawings and40 powered review drawings (39 for Velvet); Pendle has8 further reload drawings. The authoritative allocation and limitations are in README.md. The guide below records art and integration requirements, not completed directional coverage.

Priority after this pass is final contour/body-volume cleanup, matching directions, powered secondaries/traversal and editable layered masters. Preserve identities and equipment from ART_DIRECTION.md, and derive world support from the map integration owner's contract. Never reuse old prototype counts or mirrored views as evidence of completion.

## Reviewable task commands, in order

1. **“Clean Cairn's existing frames.”** Compare silhouettes and stone volumes at gameplay size. Repair changing slab shapes, joint gaps, foot sliding, held-boulder continuity and loop seams. Deliver contact sheets and normal/slow capture before adding poses.
2. **“Complete Cairn's north/south directions.”** Author matching idle, locomotion, brace and existing attack clips with explicit hand/contact anchors. Preserve action timing and collision rules. Verify that facing and effects agree in all directions.
3. **“Create editable layered masters.”** Separate body masses, equipment and effects in Pixelorama. Add animation tags, palette and pivot/anchor metadata. Rebuild exported resources reproducibly. A lightweight rig can help rigid parts; redraw expressive contacts rather than rotating one flattened cutout.
4. **“Give Nix bespoke action poses.”** Replace reused secondary/special body poses, preserve arm-side identity in every direction, and synchronise casing, emitter and Afterimage events. Review attacks with effects hidden first.
5. **“Finish one archetype at a time.”** Start with Bramble's bow and Iona's spear, then the remaining weapon, projectile and gesture archetypes. Complete primary action and directions before multiplying secondaries and forms. Share export tooling, not identical body acting.

Frame counts are estimates, never acceptance criteria. Add drawings where support, trajectory or overlap needs explanation; use unequal holds for weight and short transitions for contact.
