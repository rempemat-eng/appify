# Companions and time away

## Available now

The active companion can be the starter or a befriended visitor. Preview before switching; level, cards, hats, currency and collection stay shared. Invalid or unavailable selections fall back to the starter. Switching is blocked during missions, naps and battles.

Every companion has a temperament and an optional real-world invitation. These are descriptive attributes, not hidden bonuses. The existing family rules still apply in battle:

| Family | Existing gameplay | Real-world direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| REST | Strong against FOCUS, weak against ROAM. No damaging habit cards currently receive its +3 power bonus. | Quiet breaks, wind-down, noticing plants and small details |
| ROAM | Strong against REST, weak against FOCUS. Short Walk and Morning Walk gain 3 power. | Walks, accessible movement, a different view, outdoor discovery |
| FOCUS | Strong against ROAM, weak against REST. Stretch and Read a Chapter gain 3 power. | Reading, study, writing, drawing and making things |

All companions have equal HP at an equal level. This does not establish full balance: REST's lack of damaging habit cards needs consideration before introducing individual mechanical traits. Rarity must not become a strength tier.

## Next: let real life leave a trace

An optional mission intention could be “read”, “rest”, “move”, “create” or “just a break”. On returning, a voluntary acknowledgement could add a small pet memory: a bookmark, pressed leaf, sketch or evening lantern. Keep these choices skippable and separate from currency. Inactivity cannot establish that someone walked, read or slept; any activity acknowledgement is self-reported.

Individual bonds could grow through different experiences together, rather than repetitive feeding or mandatory streaks. Let returning players resume without an injured or abandoned pet. For protected sleep hours, reward respecting a chosen window with a capped cosmetic acknowledgement rather than rewarding ever-longer sleep. Sleep schedules need overnight-window and timezone handling before release.

Later, pets could have balanced mechanical sidegrades and characteristic world behaviours. Define equal-strength budgets and test encounters before adding bonuses. Distinguish pet behaviour from universal accessibility settings: reduced motion must work for every pet.

## Social ideas stay separate

Read-only garden snapshots are a suitable later visiting model. Persistent gifts and notes are writes, requiring recipient verification, storage, authorisation, limits and moderation. Existing player fields alone do not establish that visits or gifts need no backend work.

A cooperative grove should preserve earned contributions when someone stops. Trees with heights proportional to offline minutes are still a visual leaderboard. Prefer equally sized personal trees around a shared bloom meter; avoid last-place rows or baseline-sensitive “most improved” rankings. Keep competition optional and separate from rest or sleep.

## Research notes

- [Finch's official features](https://finchcare.com/about-finch) connect personally chosen activities with a pet adventure and a returning story. The proposed mission memories above are a NapAttack design choice, not a finding that they improve wellbeing.
- [Ryan and Deci (2000)](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf) discuss autonomy, competence and relatedness. This supports considering choice and non-controlling feedback; it does not validate a particular app mechanic.
- [Hanus and Fox (2015)](https://itl2tlfa16.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/hanusfox_15.pdf) compared two nonrandomised courses with a combined gamification intervention. It does not isolate leaderboards or prove cooperative focus mechanics work better.
- [Nintendo's dream visits](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Support/Purchases-Subscriptions/Games/How-to-Visit-Another-Player-s-Dream-Island-Animal-Crossing-New-Horizons--1821306.html) provide an offline snapshot that cannot alter the owner's real island. Leaving a persistent gift is a separate feature.
