# Review dry run: the woodland and Crossroads (25 Oct 2026)

The first run of the full review procedure (`docs/review-agents.md`, `.claude/agents/logic-auditor.md` and `detail-auditor.md`) on the world as it stands on `main` at **86e52500** ("Crossroads props as volumes"), by the chat that built the reviewers. The art under review was drawn by other chats, so the independence rule holds; the reviewer's own detectors are under test here too, and their misses are listed as such.

## What was run

- `npm run review:scene -- woodland` and `npm run review:scene -- crossroads --biome valley`: Godot 4.7.2 headless under Xvfb at 1920 x 1080 at every spawn (woodland: start, crossroads, undercroft; Crossroads: start, woodland, hut, halt, square), `--wait 4` so the damped camera has settled, plus the whole zone composed from its scene.
- `npm run review:asset -- --all --kit --record`: the asset gate on all 121 pictures the two zones place and the 10 kit ground props, which seeded `art/catalogue/` and `art/catalogue/fix-queue.json`.
- The checklists by eye on every boxed picture, on the 4x asset sheets of the Crossroads props Matt flagged, and cell by cell on the two grids logged below.
- Eastward comparison at the same character height (ours H = 74, Eastward about 54, so Eastward crops scaled 1.37x nearest): shots 12 (bench, post box, red tree, pines, rocks), 13 (lamp post with gourd, hanging pot, two trees, churns) and 17 (churns, bench, fence, rocks, bale), the boxes in `scripts/review/eastward_bands.json`.

Crops: every finding below has an enlarged crop in `artifacts/review/<zone>/<picture>_crops.png` (numbered as in `<picture>_boxed.png`) or, for assets, the 4x sheet `artifacts/review/assets/<zone>__<name>.png`. They are regenerated by the commands above and are not committed.

## Woodland

1. **Blocker, camera shows the void on entry.** A 2 s shot of a fresh start at `start` shows 60 % of the screen black outside the world while the damped camera travels in from the zone's corner; settled at 4 s it is fine. `logic.void`. Fix: snap the PhantomCamera2D to the player and the zone limits on spawn before damping (game.gd), then play-test entry from every door.
2. **Fault, staircase water banks** (`logic.water_staircase`, 6 stretches in the start view; boxes 1 to 6 in `woodland_start_boxed.png`): the stream and pool edges are equal teeth of 3 to 7 px risers. Fix: redraw banks as runs along x or y that turn at cell lines with a 1 px wobble and rounded corners (eastward-edges.md 1.4 to 1.6), or a natural curve.
3. **Fault, a ruler-straight channel** (`logic.water_straight`, the west stream, 2.7 H; a second 1.8 H stretch east of the bridge). Fix: give it bends, pools and narrows.
4. **Fault, the fall pours from nothing past turf** (`logic.water_source`, `logic.fall_face`): the west stream stops above the boulders and a fall starts below them; a quarter of its flanks are grass. Fix: carry the stream to a rock lip and drop it down a face into the pool.
5. **Fault, the fall is a flat white bar** (`detail.fall_flat`): constant width, two tones cover 59 %. Fix: wandering streaks in 4 to 5 tones, spray, a churned plunge pool.
6. **Fault, the footbridge does not reach the banks** (`logic.bridge_span`): its deck rests 6 px past the north bank and 3 px past the south (needs 7). Fix: lengthen the deck with an abutment or sleeper on dry ground at each end.
7. **Fault, flat boulders with moss hats** (`detail.flat`, 0.27 to 0.31 H^2 one-tone faces; `kit/boulder` detail bar 0.3). Fix: form shading and cluster texture (art-style.md rule 5), moss that grows into the stone, not a cap.
8. **Fault, the log runs into the oak** (`logic.intersect`, tree-4 x log-1, footprints overlap 48 px). Fix: end the log against the root flare or move it clear.
9. **Fault, the bench has no path** (`logic.reach`: 15 % of its front is path or worn ground). Fix: a worn branch from the main path to an apron under the seat (as the pilot golden does).
10. **Fault, bare trunks at the edges** (`logic.crown_cut`, 8 trees: pine-7, pine-1, pine-2 at the north edge, tree-1, wood-6, wood-10, wood-16, wood-19 at the west edge, 31 to 63 % of each crown outside the zone). Fix: move them in until the crown shows, or out so only crown shows (a forest edge two or three trees deep).
11. **Fault, crown shadows lie in the open far from their trees** (`logic.shadow_orphan`, 9 blobs, Matt's included at 775,745). One root cause: `compose.tree_shadow` throws the crown's shadow a whole crown height east and down, so it lands on open lawn away from the trunk. Fix: put the crown's shadow under the crown and round the trunk base, thrown only a little east and down (eastward-edges.md 7).
12. **Fault, head-sized mushrooms set out in rows** (`kit/mushroom_ring`: 17 world px tall, 0.67 of an adult; one cap stamp used for most caps). Fix: ankle-high mushrooms (3 to 4 world px) in clusters of varied size and age.
13. **Fault, agaves in a temperate wood** (`kit/fern`, `logic.biome`: a rosette of thick pointed leaves). Fix: draw a fern of arching fronds of small leaflets.
14. **Fault, the birch trunk is a dotted yellow line under a floating crown** (`woodland/birch`: `logic.dotted_line`, the crown hovers over it). Fix: a solid pale trunk with dark lenticels, joined to its crown.
15. **Fault, crowns are see-through contour mesh** (`detail.mesh` on every tree and bush sprite: 24 to 40 % of each crown is gaps between outlined blobs; Eastward's crowns are solid masses of lit clusters). Fix: repaint the crowns as leaf clusters with a dark interior and no outline round each blob.
16. **Fault, uniform stamped grass** (`woodland/ground`, `detail.repetition`: 1.6 exact repeats of a tuft stamp per H^2; the pilot golden 0.0, Eastward up to 0.7). Fix: several tuft designs, varied, clumped at edges and sparse where people walk.
17. **Fault, bushes on stilts and a beech crown hovering over its trunk** (`woodland/bush`, `bramble`: `logic.stilts`; `woodland/beech`: `logic.floating_part`). Fix: foliage down to a grassed base; a crown that covers its trunk top.
18. **Polish, sort points below the feet** (trees and bushes: the node origin sits 10 to 17 texels below the drawn foot; under the 0.25 H threshold, so not boxed). Fix: put origins on the foot line.
19. **Polish, the stone circle reads smooth** (`standing_stone*`: detail bar 0.30 to 0.33 of Eastward's stone band). Fix: cluster texture, chips, lichen in 4 to 5 tones.
20. **Polish, the pale stone slabs at the path's edge** are flat one-tone shapes (`detail.flat`, woodland_crossroads boxes 2 and 3).

## Crossroads (after the 86e52500 redraw)

The redraw fixed what Matt flagged in the barrel (staves, hoops, a lid in its chime: passes), the well (an elliptical coping, a lit inner wall, small deep water, a windlass with a crank), the handcart, the crates and the boarded windows; those now pass the asset gate. Still wrong:

21. **Fault, benches are still a plank on two cubes** (`bench_28`, `bench_58`: `detail.volume` on the seat's end, detail bar 0.37). Fix: legs with thickness and a lit side, a stretcher, the seat's edge thickness and its shadow on the legs.
22. **Fault, the waystone's rune reads as an interface crosshair.** A symmetric bright blue cross in a ring on the stone. **The detectors missed this** (the redrawn rune has five tones, above `detail.glyph`'s threshold): found by the checklist. Fix: cut the rune into the stone (a groove with a lit lower lip and dark upper edge), glow only inside the groove, worn and uneven.
23. **Fault, the lamp plinth is still stacked rectangles** (`lantern_dark`, `lantern_lit`: `detail.volume`, detail bar 0.33). Fix: top planes lit, chamfers, a dark east side, a base shadow.
24. **Fault, the anvil reads flat** (`anvil`: `detail.flat`, `detail.volume`, detail bar 0.38; it is lit from the right). Fix: horn, face and heel as planes lit from the upper left, a polished face highlight.
25. **Fault, the grindstone wheel is a flat disc** (`grindstone_36`: one-tone face, `detail.volume`). Fix: the wheel's rim as a band with a highlight, the face shaded as a disc seen at an angle.
26. **Fault, the trough water is a flat rectangle** (`trough_35`: `detail.water_flat`, five tones in a ruler rectangle). Fix: darker under the near coping, a waterline, a reflection of the far rim.
27. **Fault, roof holes show a window grille.** The holes in the slate roofs (north cottages, fern cottage, the thatch) show a regular orange lattice on black, which reads as a barred window, not broken rafters. Found by the checklist (no detector). Fix: rafters and battens at random breaks, slates hanging at angles, light falling in.
28. **Fault, tarps are flat striped rectangles** pasted on the roofs (green and beige, diagonal stripes, no sag or tie-downs). Found by the checklist. Fix: sagging cloth with folds lit from the upper left, ropes to pegs, the slates' edge visible under it.
29. **Fault, the hut's thatch is a flat board** (`hut`: the eave as a ruler line, `detail.flat_line`; thatch strokes in one direction with no thickness at the eave). Fix: thatch in bundles with a thick, rounded, shaded eave and a ridge.
30. **Fault, the choked well stands in a thin ring outline** drawn on the paving, which reads like a selection circle. Found by the checklist. Fix: a coping of dressed stones round it, or no ring.
31. **Fault, puddles are flat blue lozenges** with a white glint on dry paving. Found by the checklist (below the screen flat threshold). Fix: darker wet setts round a shallow puddle that shows the paving through it, a 1 px light rim.
32. **Fault, bushes on stilts** (all 14 `bush_*`: `logic.stilts`, `detail.mesh`). Fix: as woodland 15 and 17.
33. **Fault, the dug beds are dark rectangles with dashes** (`beds_dug`: `detail.volume`, `detail.mesh`, art-faults 23). Fix: raised beds with lit timber edges, soil in clods with a lit side, rows of seedlings.
34. **Fault, flower tubs are flat boxes** (`flower_tub_*`: `detail.flat`, `detail.volume`). Fix: staves or planks, a lit rim, soil, plants spilling over.
35. **Fault, an unreadable dark mottled blob** at the bottom right of the start view (around 1780, 770 to 1078 on screen): a tree crown cut by the zone edge shown as a brown and green camouflage shape (`detail.flat` box 4). Fix: identify the sprite; move it or draw its crown.
36. **Fault, bare trunks at the north edge** (`logic.crown_cut`: Oak6, Oak13, Oak21, 64 to 100 % of each crown outside the zone).
37. **Fault, uniform stamped grass** (`crossroads/ground`: `detail.repetition`, 2.1 repeats per H^2).
38. **Polish, the west door leads to a zone that does not exist yet** (`logic.wiring`: ToHalt -> halt). Known (act 1); show a closed gate meanwhile.
39. **Polish, sacks are a white blob** (`sacks_12`: `detail.volume`); **the long tree shadow south of the square** is 2.7 H long (eastward-edges.md 7: about 0.6 of the height).
