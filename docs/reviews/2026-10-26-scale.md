## Watch list (in range, at an edge)

- **Doors** are 1.27 A to the lintel, at the top of the 0.95 to 1.3 A range. Eastward's doors are 1.0 A (1.15 to 1.2 A with the frame); real doors 1.18 to 1.24 A. They read well, but no door should grow further, and a new building should aim for 85 to 90.
- **Crates** stack to 75 (1.0 A), the top of a stack's range; a single crate drawn later should be 24 to 38 tall.
- **Oaks** in both zones are 233 tall (3.15 A) and the beech 242, mid range; the willow (284) and the hollow tree (299) sit near the top. Eastward's lone maple is 2.05 A; its forest trees run off the screen. Fine for the woodland, but a tree in a village square reads better at 2.2 to 2.8 A.

## What was not covered

The detector reads the art's pixels and the plan's data; it cannot judge whether a size suits a story (a giant mushroom on purpose, a toy-sized well in a garden). Doors are measured only where a building's look is in its zone plan (the Crossroads); the Undercroft's doorways are gaps in its walls and are not sized. Windows, storeys and roofs are read from the look's numbers, not from the pixels. Eastward has no barrels, anvils, troughs, stumps or logs in its integer-scale shots; those ranges come from real size plus the chunky allowance, as the table says.
