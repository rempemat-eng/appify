# Detail audit: the Act 1 props (4 Nov 2026)

The asset gate and scene gate on draft PR #435 (`claude-a/props-act1`, head **8f560abf**), by the detail-auditor (`.claude/agents/detail-auditor.md`). The art was painted by Claude A in another chat; this reviewer drew none of it, so the independence rule holds.

**Result: 2 of 21 props pass (the buoy and the broken fence). 19 fail, 3 of them on blockers.** Claude A's first look on `contact_3x` is confirmed on every point, with one correction: the speckle is not single-pixel speckle but random value noise in 2 x 2 clusters. That noise runs through the whole set (finding 4) and is why most of the props clear the detail bar while still looking busy and flat.

## What was reviewed and how

- **Subject.** The 26 still pictures of the 21 props in `game/kit/props/art/act1/` (painted by `scripts/art/kit/sets/act1.py`), the four loops (strips `*_loop.png` and the painter's GIFs), and the two studies of `scripts/art/kit/sets/act1_study.py`. The studies were regenerated from the worktree at 8f560abf. They are byte-identical to the painter's `act1_coast_1x.png` and `act1_valley_1x.png`.
- **Calibration first.** `python3 -m scripts.review.calibrate --flagged <Matt's folder> --eastward <refs-games/eastward>` caught **38 of 38** of Matt's flagged faults. False positives: 5 on 6 clean screens and 29 on 22 clean props, against 20 in `docs/review-agents.md`. The rise is in `logic.scale` and `detail.volume` on Eastward and golden crops.
- **Asset gate.** `npm run review:asset -- game/kit/props/art/act1/<name>.png --biome valley`, with `--kind notice_board` for the timetable and `--kind boulder` for the kerbs, then `--record`. The kit props are not in `assets.KIT` and are not under `game/zones`, so the png form files them under the zone `act1`: `art/catalogue/act1/<name>.json`, one record per picture, with this review's `reviewer` verdict. Sheets: `artifacts/review/assets/act1__<name>.png` (not committed). Detector result: **15 pass, 11 fail** (1 blocker, 13 faults, 19 polish).
- **Scene gate.** `npm run review:scene -- <study>.png --scale 1 --H 74 --biome valley` on both studies gave **0 findings each**. Every cell of both 6 x 4 grids was opened; the log is below.
- **By eye.** The checklist was run part by part on 6x views of every still, each beside a map of its lone texels. The 6x views are in the scratchpad and are not committed.
- **Two measurements of my own.**
  - Lone texels: a texel unlike all four of its neighbours, as a share of the interior.
  - Cluster noise: the share of 2 x 2 blocks whose mean luminance is more than 10 L from the median of their 3 x 3 block neighbourhood. This was measured inside our sprites and on Eastward's prop boxes, both at our character height.

## Findings, ranked

Severity: **blocker** (cannot be placed as it stands), **fault** (fails the gate), **polish**. Every location is the picture and its box in art px; crops are the 4x sheets `artifacts/review/assets/act1__<name>.png` (regenerate with the commands above; not committed).

1. **Blocker. The skep reads as an upside-down cone.** `hive_skep` (0,0,29,51).
   - **What is wrong.** `act1.skep` centres the skep's ellipsoid at z 12 on the stool's top, with a vertical radius of 18. So its lower half (z -6 to 12) hangs in front of the stool, and the skep's widest point is halfway up. At 1x in the valley study (cell r2c1) it reads as a golden carrot standing on its point.
   - **Fix: structure.** A skep is a bell, widest at its foot. Use a half-ellipsoid from z 12 (the stool's top) up to z 28, radius 10 to 11 at the foot, narrowing to a domed crown.
   - **Fix: material.** Draw the coils as rings lit on their top edge (straw 4 over 3, with a dark 2 line under each ring). Add the binding as short timber_cool 2 stitches staggered from ring to ring. Put a dark entrance notch at the foot, on the south.
   - **Fix: hackle.** A conical straw hackle over the top, lit on its west flank.
   - **Fix: shading.** Keep the stool visible under the skep's foot, with a contact line.

2. **Blocker. The waiting shelter is a third of its size.** `halt_waiting_shelter` (0,0,92,81).
   - **Size.** The eave is at z 46 (1.06 m). The size guide gives rain-shelter eaves of 40 to 48 world px, so 120 to 144 art px, and the painter's own metadata says 2.2 m (96 px). Bramble (74) would stand with his head through the roof.
   - **Fix: size.** Raise the back wall, sides and roof to an eave of 120 to 130 art px. Keep the bench seat at about 0.46 A (34 px, as Eastward's bench).
   - **Fix: roof structure.** The roof is a single flat slab of horizontal lines. As a lean-to it needs:
     - a visible thickness at the eave, 3 to 4 px, lit along its top edge;
     - a fascia board with a lit lip;
     - a half-round gutter, with a highlight on its upper left;
     - the slates in staggered rows, each row's lower edge lit (house-style.md 5, roofs; art-style.md rule 6);
     - a 2-row eave shadow on the back wall.
   - **Fix: fretted valance.** It is a mottled band. Give it a scalloped lower edge, in cream 4 with cream 2 in the cut-outs.
   - **Fix: bench and poster.** The bench is mottle. Use boards, as the style-anchor bench: 4 to 5 px boards, a dark joint, a lit lip, 2 x 6 grain streaks and nail heads. The poster is a flat red train glyph; make it a sheet with a printed block and a torn corner.
   - **Reference.** The anchor `kit/bench`; Eastward's bench in 12 (180,200,248,235).

3. **Blocker. The harbour light is a lighthouse model.** `coast_harbour_light` (0,0,28,114). See the table above.
   - **Fix: what it is.** Make it what the piece and the backlog say: a quay-head harbour light.
   - **Fix: structure.**
     - A cast-iron lamp column on a granite plinth, in the lamp class (107 to 178 art px). The column is fluted, 6 to 8 px across.
     - A lantern on top: a glazed box of 12 to 14 px with an iron frame, a red or green lens, and a domed cap with a vent and finial.
     - A short ladder bar under the lantern.
     - No door and no gallery. A door and gallery belong only to a tower in the tower class, with a door of 70 to 96 px.
     - If the design must stay a banded tower, it becomes the lighthouse: at least 312 art px, a door of 70 px or more, a gallery rail about 27 px high, on rock with a keeper's cottage (size-guide.md, "Lookout tower, lighthouse").
   - **Fix: whitewash.** It carries the set's grain (finding 4); paint it as a calm, lit plane. Give the column a cylinder: a highlight band left of centre in white 5 over 4, a core shadow in white 1 to 2 on the right third. Add 2 or 3 rust streaks running down from the gallery or lantern, 1 x 4 to 6 px.
   - **Reference.** Eastward's lamp post in 13 (375,20,415,125) for the iron and the lantern.

4. **Fault (the whole set). Random value noise in 2 x 2 clusters replaces material.**
   - **Where.** Every picture. Worst: `mill_landslip`, `halt_platform_edge`, `coast_net_rack`, `hive_wbc`, `mill_millstones`, `halt_waiting_shelter` (roof, bench), `coast_harbour_light` (shaft), `mill_sluice` (piers), `halt_timetable_board` (hood, posts).
   - **What is wrong.** `_rich` (act1.py) adds `grain * G`, where G is +1 on 20 % and -1 on 22 % of 2 x 2 cells, chosen by hash and independent of form. So 42 % of every surface steps a tone at random. house-style.md 4 says the opposite: "most clusters only swap to a sibling ramp at the same tone (they add shape without value noise); a few step one tone up (more on the lit side) or down".
   - **Measured.** A median 44.8 % of blocks stand out, against Eastward's 27.8 % and the style anchors' 28 to 32 %. Side by side, Eastward's stone and painted surfaces (the post box in 12, the rocks in 17) are smooth planes with deliberate edges and parts. Ours are terrazzo.
   - **Fix.** Drop the ±1 grain to sibling swaps at the same tone on most cells. Allow tone steps on at most 10 % of cells, +1 only on lit planes and -1 only in shade. Then draw texture per material, as the recipes say:
     - **wood:** grain streaks 2 x 4 to 8 along the board, a knot now and then;
     - **stone:** parts 8 to 15 px with chipped corners and mortar, cracks, moss in the low joints;
     - **whitewash or paint:** a calm plane with weathering streaks running down from ledges;
     - **earth:** clods in drifts with lit tops and shadowed undersides.
   - **Target.** Cluster noise at most 30 % with the bar still met. The bar must be earned by parts, not grain.

5. **Fault. The timetable board is a UI glyph grid.** `halt_timetable_board` (3,10,52,60).
   - **What is wrong.** Five identical rows of square, arrow, green bar, red bar and blue square: a spreadsheet of icons (`detail.glyph` did not fire).
   - **Fix: the sheet.** Make it a printed sheet behind glass:
     - a header band holding a small train and clock pictogram;
     - rows of grey print as broken dashes of varying lengths (plaster 2 on plaster 4), not identical icons;
     - one pinned notice of a different size, set at a slight angle;
     - a diagonal glass glint in 2 px steps at the upper left (house-style.md 5, glass).
   - **Fix: hood.** It is a mottled slab. Give it a small pitched roof with a lit west plane, a shaded east plane and a drip edge.
   - **Fix: posts.** They are camouflage mottle. Paint each post with a lit left column (green 4), a body (green 3) and a dark right column (green 1 to 2).

7. **Fault. The churns are bottles.** `halt_churn_stand` (2,1,57,55).

   - **Fix: shape.** Add a rolling band at the base and at the shoulder (lit top line, dark under line), a neck, a mushroom lid with a handle loop, and two side handles.
   - **Fix: tin shading.** A highlight band left of centre (snow 5 over 4) and a core shadow on the right third, never a diagonal split.
   - **Reference.** Churns in 17 (135,47,162,112).

8. **Fault. Old Fang's barrow: wallpaper turf, a flat portal, and a third of its own size.** `barrow_entrance` (0,0,142,103).
   - **Turf.** The mound is covered in the same 3 to 4 px dark dashes at an even density everywhere (the `detail.mesh` lead), over 2 x 2 mottle, so the dome's light is lost.
   - **Portal.** The doorway is a flat indigo rectangle: no reveal, no darkening into the passage, no threshold stone. The jambs are overgrown into mottle, so the stones do not read.
   - **Size.**

   - **Fix: size.** The portal at least 40 x 74 px. The mound in proportion: at least 300 px long and 70 to 90 px high, or split into a front piece and mound cells.
   - **Fix: dome light.** Light the dome in bands (grass 4 crown at the upper left, 3 body, 2 east and lower flank). Put sparse clumped tufts on top, not a dash wallpaper.
   - **Fix: portal stones.** Two jamb stones and a lintel, each with a lit top-left plane and a dark right face, and moss in the joints only.
   - **Fix: passage.** Make it recede: the inner reveal lit on its east wall, darkness grading to void 0 within 6 to 8 px, and a threshold slab.
