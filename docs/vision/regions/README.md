# Region briefs: the hand-over from the world plan

One brief per region and owner, written from the world plan v5.3 (`../world-plan.md`, PR #96) so each lane can build as soon as Matt approves the plan. Each brief gives the region's footprint in world pixels, its places and why they are there, its transitions, its tiers, its content placed with coordinates, its boundaries in layers, its obstacles and loops, the benchmark checks to hold on drawn art, the kits to use and today's zones it replaces or keeps.

**Shared rules.** World pixels on the 11264 x 6912 px canvas, 8 px cells, one screen 320 x 576 px. Paths first; the ground fitted to each route. Transitions as places. Edges in three layers, drawn as land. No reward, cost or chore from decoration. The benchmark (`../research/world-benchmark.md`) is the bar, held again on the drawn art. Lanes edit only the files the lane table in `../coherence-plan.md` gives them; this folder is Claude A's.
