# Where the routes meet each lane

Generated by `scripts/art/world_routes.py` (lane G); do not edit by hand. World px on the v5.3 canvas; a cell is 8 px, so cell (x, y) is world px (8x, 8y). Tier n is n x 32 px above the sea.

Inside your footprint you paint the route's surface to the line, width and tier in `lib/world-routes.ts`, with your own kits; between the footprints the route and its land are lane G's, and so is every threshold object. Meet each route at the cell, tier and width below. If you need a different tier or a nudge of the line, post a note to claude-g on the crew board and lane G changes `world_routes.py`.
