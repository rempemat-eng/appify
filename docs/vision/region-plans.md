# Region plans: a region laid out as data before it is painted

Claude F, 21 Oct (lane F: the Frostline, the massif and the Brackens). Matt, 21 Oct 10:03: speed up world building while staying accurate to the rules. A region's layout is written once, as data, in `art/regions/<id>.json`, and everything else reads it: the chunk painter, Claude G's routes (where they enter and leave), the set-piece placement library (Codex C36) and the checks. A mistake in the layout is caught before any art is drawn.

## What a plan holds

All points are world px on the v5.3 canvas (11264 x 6912), on the 8 px grid for structure.

| Field                                                   | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `box`                                                   | the region's footprint from its brief, snapped to the 8 px grid                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `base_tier`                                             | which world tier the plan's tier 0 is; provisional until the geography chunk (world plan P1) fixes heights                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `tiers`                                                 | outlines in screen space, along x or y only and turning at cell corners (the grid shape, Matt 21 Oct 11:44): a tier's top where the camera sees it, its south face hanging 32 px a tier under its south rim (64 where two tiers drop at once), side bands where it ends west or east; no tab or notch of 1 to 2 cells; `corners`: `rounded` (the default, drawn by the kit) or `square` (a built edge) |
| `routes`, `trods`                                       | polylines with a width (24 to 64); routes are Claude G's chunks and are drawn here path first, trods are the region's own side paths, pockets and loops                                                                                                                              |
| `stairs`                                                | where a path changes tier: on the path, at least 34 px wide (eastward-edges 6: no ramps)                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `water`                                                 | tarns as polygons, streams as polylines with their falls                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `named`, `pockets`, `markers`, `egg_spots`, `obstacles` | the brief's section 5 and 7, moved to fit the ground where needed (`moved_from` keeps the brief's point)                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `set_pieces`                                            | the cliff core's pieces as `{piece, biome, at, seed}`, the shape of C36's placement file; a biome uses only its own pieces (to-all-reuse-rule)                                                                                                                                       |
| `areas`                                                 | the region's screens (about 320 x 544, whole 32 px audit cells of the box): the places the world audit counts density in; `walkable: false` marks drawn land never walked (the massif's front)                                                                                       |
| `edges`, `thresholds`, `residents`, `ground`            | the layered borders, the thresholds' sides and bands, who lives or crosses where, and the ground kinds by place                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `water` (more)                                          | `source` and `outlet` (a name or a point; a stream leaving the box names where it goes) or `declared_closed: true`; a closed body's `islands` (`{outline}`), `bridges` (`{at, span}`); a path's `ford` or `fords` (points where it wades) |
| `coast`                                                 | the land's outline as one polygon, or `{name: polygon}` for several islands (strings there are notes); outside it is sea |
| `buildings`                                             | `{id, foot: [x, y] its south-west corner, w}`: footprints nothing may stand in (a door marker stands at its building) |
| `in_water`                                              | on a named thing, pocket, marker or resident that stands in the shallows or on stilts by design |
| `camps`                                                 | the Godot game's fights (docs/godot.md, Camps): `{id, arena: [x, y, w, h], enemies: [[x, y], ...], cover: [[x, y], ...], boss?, why}`; the arena is at least 96 x 64 world px, holds its enemies and is touched by a path |
| `rails[]` (more)                                        | `crossings` (`{with, at}`), `inclines` (`{at, kind}`: cutting, embankment or rope incline), `buffers`, `viaduct`, `tunnel` |

## The check

`npm run check:region-plans` (`scripts/art/region_plan.py --check`, part of `npm run check`) holds every plan to the brief's rules that can be checked before art exists: structure on the grid and inside the box; tier outlines with no tab or notch of 1 to 2 cells (a finger or slot whose end edge is 16 px or less; a rectangle is exempt; masses step in and out by more, to-all-grid-2x, a-to-f-grid), and corners `rounded` or `square`; path widths; every path segment along x or y, turning at cell corners (the grid shape, which replaces benchmark 16's bend every 240 to 300 px); dead ends of 80 px or more ending in a view, a resident, a bench or a story detail (20); every change of tier on a stair (19, 21) and no path along a face; named things, pockets and markers on walkable ground beside their path, never on it or on a stair; egg spots kept clear; obstacles 800 px apart; the markers' density per screen of route and per screen area (1); set pieces from the cliff core's library (`stair`, `ledge`, `path`, `spring`, `pond`, `snow_corner`, `boulders`, `scree`, `ivy`) or marked `proposed`. Three more checks from Claude I's `lull_delta.py` (#177): placement (nothing placed stands in water, in the sea outside the `coast`, or in a building's footprint, unless it is on a bridge, a deck span, an island or marked `in_water`); reach (a flood fill at 4 px from every route's ends over walkable ground, changing tier only on a stair and crossing water only on a bridge, causeway, jetty, boardwalk, slipway, ford or island, faults every placed thing and egg spot it cannot reach; land in a `walkable: false` area is not walked); and water ends (every water body has a `source` and an `outlet`, or is `declared_closed`). Lane R's reviews (22 and 23 Oct) added: water crossings (a route or trod crossing open water has a bridge, ford, declared crossing or deck within half the water's width plus 16 px); water height (a stream never rises from source to outlet, and every drop has a fall within 48 px); rails (ends at a stop, buffer, rail or the box edge, crossings declared, every tier change on an incline, a shared rail with one line); edge meets (a path ending on the box edge meets a neighbour's path or a lane G route within 16 px). And camps (an arena on the ground, holding its enemies, touched by a path; the enemies reached like any placed thing). `python3 scripts/art/region_plan.py OUT_DIR` draws each plan (a world px is a quarter pixel) to check it by eye; `--fix` moves a thing that breaks a placement rule to the nearest point that keeps them all, keeping the brief's point as `moved_from`; `--grid` turns slanted outline edges and path segments into steps along x and y and closes tabs and notches (each finger cut back to its shorter side, unless that would take more than a tenth of the tier, which the check then leaves for a person); the check also prints a note, not a fault, where a tier's east-west edge runs more than a screen width (320 px) without stepping in or out (to-all-volume); `--dress` adds dressing slots (`dress: true`) along the paths so every screen area has its 2 interactables and 3 story props, then settles them.

It does not judge art: the painted chunk is held to the world audit (`npm run check:world-audit`) and walked in the browser at 360 px.
