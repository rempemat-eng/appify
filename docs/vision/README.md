# NapAttack vision

This folder is the shared brief for anyone building NapAttack: Matt, Claude and Codex. Read it before any design, art or interface work, and update it when the direction changes. When this folder and an older doc disagree, this folder wins.

**Read [game-design.md](game-design.md) first** (21 Oct). Matt turned the game into an action RPG and village life sim with a story that grows into a light looter-shooter: real-time combat after Eastward, quests, levelling, a village to build, arenas redone for the new combat, and no rest, naps, dreams, screen time or Dream Dust anywhere. Where any doc below disagrees with it, game-design.md wins; the docs it replaces carry a "Superseded 21 Oct" banner. The world, regions, train network, cast and 2x art rules below all stand.

## Matt's direction (October 2026)

- **An action RPG and village life sim (21 Oct 21:01 and 21:16).** "completely remove the screen tied mechanics ... introduce the direction action (eastward type) combat system"; "completely get rid of the rest/ dream idea. make it into an action rpg/ life sim with a story but evolving into a bit of a looter shooter (but not too massively)". See [game-design.md](game-design.md). Round 2 (22:05 and 22:09): Crossroads restored as the village, the familiar backs you instead of fighting, and **landscape only, desktop first, phones second** ("the screen should be horizontal on mobile or desktop. desktop first, mobile second"). The rest, nap, dream, screen-time and phone-first bullets below are superseded by it; the rest stand.
- **One game, not an app with a game in it.** The world fills the phone. Controls sit on top of it (a HUD, a hotbar, a menu) or live inside it (tap the signpost, the mailbox, the bed). Home does not scroll.
- **Pixel everything.** Buttons, panels and indicators use hard-edged pixel frames, not rounded web controls. All text uses one pixel font.
- **Clean, detailed art with real shading.** "Shading is super important. I like it clean." Outlines stay. Objects should feel 3D: a lit side, a shaded side, cast shadows. The bar is the references in art-style.md.
- **Cosy tone.** Gentle, warm, never shaming (kept on 21 Oct as a warm tone with real stakes). The companion encourages; it does not nag.
- **The garden grows** (superseded 21 Oct: the village grows by what you build). Time away visibly changes the world.
- **Intuitive and immersive.** Show, do not explain. Guidance appears in the world (a "!" over the next place, the companion speaking) rather than as cards of text.
- **Content after polish.** Once the look and feel are right, add more places, visitors and things to do.
- **A living world (17 Oct; its currency, decay and time-away rules superseded 21 Oct).** Matt approved the merged plan and Codex's living-world ideas: a safe currency earned away from the phone, gentle reversible decay, seasons and weather as scenery, people with routines, decorating and interiors, lasting story changes and new biomes. Order and rules: [living-world-plan.md](living-world-plan.md).
- **Art density 2 (21 Oct 18:17).** Matt: "let's go with 2x. final decision." All new art is drawn at 2x (an adult 50 art px, a cell 16, 600 across); world logic stays in world px. See [art-style.md](art-style.md) and [size-guide.md](size-guide.md), "Density 2".
- **GRASS is now Dream Dust (20 Oct; superseded 21 Oct: the currency is to be Glint, game-design.md section 10).** Matt, 20 Oct 21:29: "rebrand grass currency into dream dust". The currency is called Dream Dust everywhere a player sees it ("DREAM DUST" on caps labels, "Dream Dust" in sentences) and its icon is a stoppered vial of glowing dust (`dreamDust` in `scripts/art/icons.py`). Only the name and the icon changed: the amounts and rules are the same, and the saved and database fields keep their old names (`grass`, `offGrass`, `grass_amount`) so every existing save loads the same balance. Grass the plant, "Touch grass together" and the Grasslings keep their names.

**World rules** (Matt, 18 Oct): the world must make sense: water has a source and a sink and only floating or supported things stand on it, paths lead to places, gates sit in fences, neighbouring places are built together with corridors between them, and detail follows from these rules. The research is [../research/2d-world-design.md](../research/2d-world-design.md); the rules are [world-rules.md](world-rules.md).

**World atlas** (Matt, 17 Oct): the regions beyond the valley (Driftwood Harbour, Hearthold Castle, steampunk Cogsworth Junction, the western Dust Gulch, the Ember Caldera, cyberpunk Neon Harbour, Halcyon Station and the moon Lumen), the theme that tied them together (a journey from rest to screens and back to sleep, superseded 21 Oct by the starfall in game-design.md), seamless travel and a world that feels alive: [world-atlas.md](world-atlas.md).

## Working agreements

- Edit the generators in `scripts/art/` and rerun `npm run art`; never hand-edit generated PNGs or `lib/garden-map.ts`.
- New colours go into `art/materials.json` (shared ramps), not into individual files.
- Keep the source-level harnesses in `scripts/check-*.cjs` meaningful: derive expectations from generated data (the map, the ramps) rather than hard-coding coordinates.
- Play-test in a browser at phone size (402×874) and look at screenshots before calling art or layout done.
