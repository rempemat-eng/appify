# Size guide: how large everything in the world is

Matt, 20 Oct 10:58: "make sure you establish the sizing and soft grid for all regions and a guide on how large props, buildings etc. should be and also continue working on the logic of everything (improve the rules of the world as you build). the world should feel expansive with natural looking boundaries and no abrupt cuts".

This is the one reference every lane draws and places by. For every kind of thing in the world it gives a size in zone px: height, width, footprint in 8 px cells, where its anchor sits, the size of its door or opening, and the ground kept clear in front of it, each with a reason. Section 0 is the one-page quick table. The rules that use these sizes are in [world-rules.md](world-rules.md) (v2); the look is in `house-style.md` (draft PR #121) and [art-style.md](art-style.md).

**Where the numbers come from.** Decided sizes are taken as they stand and marked _(decided)_: the scale table in `verticality.md` 5 (an adult is 25 px, a door 32, a storey 32, a shop storey 44), the soft grid and its five rules in `verticality.md` 4f and `research/soft-grid.md`, and the sizing table in `world-plan.md` 1.4 (Matt decided v2's decisions 1 to 7 on 20 Oct). The continent, its routes, its transitions and the rocket come from `world-plan.md` v3 (20 Oct, on `claude-a/world-plan`), whose decisions 8 to 19 are recommended and not yet decided; sizes taken from it are marked _(plan v3)_. Material sizes (bricks, courses, boards) follow `house-style.md` (draft PR #121; Matt approved its bottom row as the target for each setting, 20 Oct 11:20). Gaps are filled from the reference measurements in `research/world-references.md` and `world-plan.md` 1.2 (official screenshots, measured by character height) or, where no shot shows the thing, from real-world sizes at 15 px a metre, marked _(inferred)_. Inferred sizes are this guide's proposal: they hold until play or Matt says otherwise.

**The data.** The kinds that a sprite can be measured against live in `scripts/art/size_guide.json` beside this file, with the name map from every prop sprite to its kind. `python3 scripts/art/size_check.py` measures every sprite in `assets/zones/props` against it (report only, section 10). Change this file and the JSON together; the appendix table is printed from the JSON by `size_check.py --markdown`.

## 1. Units and principles

- **The adult is the unit.** A = 25 px, feet to crown _(decided)_. The companion (24 px) is a small pet drawn large for readability; it is not the yardstick _(decided: world-plan v2 decision 12, v3 decision 19)_.
- **The grid is 8 px**, a third of an adult; the walk grid is 4 px (2 x 2 walk cells to a grid cell) _(decided)_. Every footprint, rim, path edge and stair is a whole number of cells; heights are not on the grid but come from this table.
- **15 px a metre** for anything this guide fills in from real life (A = 25 px is a 1.7 m adult) _(inferred)_.
- **Three scales, on purpose.** What people touch and use is true size: doors, counters, seats, crates, carts, cars, boats, animals (at 15 px a metre). Buildings are compressed: a storey is 32 (2.1 m) instead of a real 3 m, and a cottage stands on 80 x 48 instead of 8 x 6 m, so a street holds the right number of houses on a phone (`verticality.md` 5: "fewer storeys, not squashed ones"). Landscape is compressed most: a mature oak is 90 (6 m) instead of 20 m, a river 40 to 64. Why: the phone shows 12 adults across, as Eastward (12.8) and Stardew (15) do; at true scale a tree would fill the screen and a village would be ten screens wide.
- **Small animals** may be drawn up to twice true size so they read at 1.3 to 2 screen pixels a world pixel, but never taller than half an adult (12 px) _(inferred, from Stardew's hens at half a farmer)_.
- **The anchor is the foot.** A built prop's foot line lies on a grid row, its west edge on a grid column (or its centre on a cell centre for odd widths); the sprite is drawn up from the foot and sorts by foot y _(decided: soft-grid rule 4)_. Only vegetation and ground dressing are free (they jitter 0 to 7 px; their base cell still checks).
- **A footprint lies on one tier**, never on a face, a stair, a landing or a lip, and stands 1 cell back from a lip unless it is a railing _(decided: prop-logic 6)_.
- **Ranges are for variety.** Each kind has a range so that neighbours differ (no two alike); the target is the middle.
- **What is kept clear** (prop-logic rule 5 and section 6): a door apron 4 x 2 cells on the door's tier with a path or worn ground leading away; a cart door 6 x 3; a stall's customer band 6 x 2; a bench's front 4 x 2; a well's ring 1 cell all round; stair landings 8 px deep; the walking band of every path; and in every area one mostly empty node of at least 6 x 6 cells (48 x 48).
- **A hit box is not the sprite.** Every tappable prop declares a hit box of at least 34 x 34 world px at its foot (44 screen px; 68 x 68 art px at 2x), apart from other hit boxes at standing distance; a true-size door (14) or crate (11) is narrower than that, and scale fixes never shrink a hit area _(decided)_.

## 10. How it is checked

- **`scripts/art/size_check.py`** (this PR, report only) measures every sprite in `assets/zones/props` (the trimmed box of pixels at least half opaque, at 1x) against its kind's height and width ranges in `scripts/art/size_guide.json`, with 2 px of tolerance for the outline, and prints findings per lane. It cannot see doors, storeys or counters inside a sprite, nor placement. A sprite that is a group, a part or an effect is listed as unmeasured with a reason; a sprite with no entry is listed so it gets one.
- **`prop-scale`** (proposed in `verticality.md` 5, Claude A, not built): each prop module declares `KIND`, `DOOR_RECTS` and `STOREYS`, and `world_lint.py` measures door and storey heights too. When it lands, it reads the same JSON, and `size_check.py` becomes its sprite-box half.
- **Footprints and clearances** (`prop-snap`, `prop-footprint`, `apron-clear`, proposed in `research/soft-grid.md` 6.5 and `prop-logic.md` 9) read the `foot` column here once modules declare `FOOT` and `APRON`.
- **The line-up** stays the acceptance: the sprite at 3x beside a resident, then the zone at phone scale (`verticality.md` 5).

Each owner fixes these in the generator, never by stretching a bitmap (prop-logic rule 9), and re-runs the check.
