## Comparison: Sunstone's two railways against Stardew Valley's Railroad and Pokémon's Magnet Train

Earlier runs compared:

- the coast, the isles, Neon and Cogsworth;
- the Frostline, the town, Driftwood, Hearthold and the Woodland;
- the home garden and the Brackens.

This run takes Sunstone's rail lines: the desert line to Dust Gulch and the mine cart from the Lode. Both are drawn as plans, not painted yet.

I have not played these games for this note, and the claims stay at what the sources say.

### Sources

- **Stardew Valley,** [the Railroad page on the official wiki](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Railroad):
  - "The Train Platform is located north of the tracks."
  - The trains come through a tunnel ("Standing inside the right side of the train tunnel" can trap the player).
  - The place around them has its own uses: the Spa in the south-west, and a cave north-east of the platform.
- **Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver,** [the Magnet Train page on Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Magnet_Train):
  - the train links two stations, "Goldenrod City in Johto and Saffron City in Kanto";
  - its tracks "are visible on Route 32, where they run overhead", and are not shown on the other routes.

### What reads better there

- **One line, one platform beside it.** Stardew's railroad is a single line with the platform on one side, so the player never has to read which track goes where. Sunstone has two lines meeting at Dust Gulch, crossing each other 56 px from the buffer (finding 3).
- **Height is shown by a structure.** Where Pokémon's line meets a walkable route, it runs overhead. It neither meets the path at grade nor climbs the ground. Sunstone's railway climbs five tiers with no cutting or embankment (finding 7), and its halt in the valley has the road across the platform (finding 8).
- **A line leaves the screen through a portal.** Stardew's trains enter by a tunnel. Sunstone's mine cart already does this well at the forge: `mine_cart_tunnel` ends 4 px from the cart bay. But its other copy in the fire country ends in a diagonal that leads nowhere (finding 4).

### What Sunstone should do (Claude D; no new rules)

1. **Separate the lines at Dust Gulch.** End the mine cart in its own bay east of the railway's buffer, with the dry forecourt between them that S1 asks for. Nothing crosses the railway's last 96 px.
2. **Give every change of height a visible structure.** A cutting through the tier where the desert line climbs out of the Rift (tier 4 to 5), an embankment with a culvert at the Willow Run, and a rope incline with a winding house at the Lode for the cart. Each is a set piece the painter can draw, and each says why the line is where it is.
3. **Use the tunnel portal twice.** The cart already leaves the forge by a tunnel. Give the Lode end a matching portal at the mine mouth (3040, 1648), so the line reads as one thing from mouth to bay.
