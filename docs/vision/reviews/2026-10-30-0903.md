## Findings, most severe first

1. **`--camp` crashes the camp spawn** (owner: codex; `game/game.gd:191`). `String(args.camp).split(",")` raises "Invalid call. Nonexistent 'String' constructor" when `--camp` is a bare flag (args value is `true`/empty). The camp still appears but the script error fails any check that greps for `SCRIPT ERROR`. Use `str(args.camp)`.
2. **Quest wiring: 40 events can never fire** (owner: claude-k; `game/data/quests/**`). `review:scene` reports the same 40 `logic.wiring` blockers on every zone (88 rows in each report because of duplicates). Examples: `a1_the_circle_wakes` waits for `talk:circle_stones`; `a1_a_cup_by_the_stove` for `talk:hut_warden`; `a1_the_landslip` for `camp:millrace_slip`; `a1_the_cut_rope` for `enter:the_barrow`; others name `fells_ibex`, `ice_caves_rooms`, `driftwood_quay`. Most are future zones, but `circle_stones` and `hut_warden` belong to the woodland and hut, which exist or are planned now. Either place the speakers or mark those quests as gated on their zone so the gate stops blocking.
3. **Woodland, black rectangle beside the signpost** (owner: claude-a, woodland; screenshot ~(660,180) of 1920x1080 at the `stones` spawn, just above the sign). A flat dark 30x25 px block with no art, reads as a missing texture or hole.

No forge, anvil, smoke or lit window appears, so the restoration does not read as a payoff. The plaza is a large empty stone slab with two puddles and two benches.
