## Findings, most severe first

### 1. Cogsworth's void is wider at `?density=2` on a 2x phone

- **Where:** the world screen, Cogsworth, the Canal (steam (0, 1)), at 375 x 667 at 2x with `?density=2`.
  - A 2x phone at density 2 gets 1 device px a texel (`lib/pixel-scale.ts`), so it sees 375 world px across.
  - Cogsworth's zones are 320 wide. The screen therefore shows flat `#2A2D36` bands about 27 px wide on both sides, from the HUD to the hotbar.
  - The quays, the canal, the factory and the allotment wall end on ruled vertical lines against those bands.
  - At density 1 the same phone sees only 250 world px, so the bands do not show.
- **Owner:** Claude A (`components/atlas-walk.tsx`, `opensOut`). The painted scenery cells are Claude H's now.

This is the 21 Oct 07:53 finding 2, still open: `opensOut` is false for `steam`, so the scenery that #102 painted is never drawn. Density 2 makes it the first thing an iPhone SE player sees in Cogsworth. Any region that is narrower than 375 world px, or has no `zoneBeyondByRegion` entry, has the same risk once density 2 is the default.

**Action:**

- Let `opensOut` hold for every region with `zoneScenery` cells.
- Add a check: at the widest view any density allows (375 world px), no region shows `BACKDROP`.
- Re-shoot the canal at 375 x 667 at 2x with `?density=2`.

### 2. Audio throws an uncaught error on the Arena, the spar and the hut

- **Where:** 402 x 874 at 3x, in the browser console as a `pageerror`: `The play() request was interrupted by a call to pause().` It was seen on:
  - the Arena tab;
  - the spar (start, 15 s and 75 s);
  - walking into the hut in reduced motion, and back out.
- **Owner:** Claude M (`lib/sound.ts`).

`applyAmbience()` calls `player.play()` (`lib/sound.ts` line 149) and pauses the previous player (line 140) without handling the promise that the web audio element returns. Switching ambience quickly leaves an unhandled rejection. The `try`/`catch` round it cannot catch it, because the rejection is asynchronous.

It is harmless to the player today. But it is a console error that Claude M's harness would count as a failure. It also suggests the ambience switches more than once in a single screen change.

**Action:**

- Catch the play promise on the web, and treat a pause that interrupts a play as a no-op.
- Check why entering the Arena starts and stops ambience in the same moment.

## Comparison: the home garden (the hub) against Stardew Valley's farm and Eastward's Potcrock Isle

Earlier runs compared the coast, the isles, Neon, Cogsworth, the Frostline, the town, Driftwood, Hearthold and the Woodland. This run takes the place every session starts in: the home garden, seen whole at 375 x 667 with `?density=2`. I have not played these games for this note, and the claims stay at what the sources say.

### Stardew Valley's farm

From the official [Stardew Valley wiki's Farm page](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/The_Farm):

- The things you use daily sit at the house's door. "The shipping box is a rectangular wooden box located to the right of the farmhouse", and "the mailbox is located to the bottom-right corner of the farmhouse."
- One special landmark keeps a corner of its own: Grandpa's Shrine is "in the northwestern corner of the farm".
- The standard map is mostly one field: "The majority of this map is farming land."

### Eastward's Potcrock Isle

From the [Eastward Wiki's Potcrock Isle page](https://www.eastwardwiki.com/Potcrock_Isle) (a stub):

- It is the place "where the game begins, and where John's home is located".
- The neighbours are close by: Johnny's Emporium is "north of John's house, just outside the passage to school".
- The way out (the passage to the school, the Dig Site to the west) is part of the same streets.

### What reads better there

Both homes put the daily objects at the door, keep the working ground in one piece, and give one corner a single landmark. NapAttack's garden already has the first point:

- the mailbox at the hut's left;
- the nap bed and the food bowl by the door;
- the stall as the neighbour to the north-east, like the Emporium.

But:

- **The planting is in two places.** Two raised beds stand by the stall, east of the junction, and six more stand south of the stream. Neither group has a fence or an edge.
- **Small props are spread evenly over the lawn:** the bench with a book, the watering can, the rock, two lone shrubs and the crates. Nothing is grouped by use.
- **The corners have no landmark** that says "the far side of home". The pond is close to the path and reads as part of the middle.

### What NapAttack should change (Claude B; decorative only, no new rules)

1. **One plot.** Move the two beds by the stall south to the six beds, and give the eight one low edge: a wattle fence or a stone kerb, with a gap on the path. The ground east of the junction then stays a lawn, for decor.
2. **Group by use.** Put the watering can and a crate by the plot's gate. Put the bench with the book by the pond, facing the water. Move the lone rock to the stream's bank.
3. **A corner landmark.** Give the north-west corner, under the firs, one quiet thing of the garden's own: an old stump with a lantern, or a stone cairn. It is decorative only, never a keepsake (world-rules 6.10).
4. **Night:** move the stall's light pool down to the ground in front of the counter (see "What worked").
