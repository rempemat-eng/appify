## Comparison: Cinder Springs against Lavaridge Town and Stardew Valley's Spa

Earlier runs compared:

- the coast, the isles, Neon and Cogsworth;
- the Frostline, the town, Driftwood, Hearthold and the Woodland;
- the home garden, the Brackens and Sunstone's rail lines.

This run takes Cinder Springs in the fire country, which is drawn as a plan and not painted yet.

I have not played these games for this note, and the claims stay at what the sources say.

### Sources

- **Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald,** [Lavaridge Town on Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lavaridge_Town):
  - "The hot springs are connected to an opening in the back of the Pokémon Center";
  - an old woman stands "by the sand baths".
- **Stardew Valley,** [the Spa on the official wiki](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Spa):
  - it is "located in the southwest area of the Railroad";
  - two changing rooms lead by a covered path to the pool.

### What reads better there

- **The bath and what serves it share one floor.** In both games the building that serves the water (the Pokémon Center's back door, the Spa's changing rooms) opens straight onto the pool's edge. Cinder Springs puts its towels, rail and bench a cliff below the pools (finding 6).
- **The water is big enough to be in.** Both pools are meant to be entered, and they are drawn to hold several people. At 48 x 24 and 40 x 16, Cinder Springs' pools are puddles beside a 25 px adult.
- **The springs belong to a place.** Lavaridge's springs are part of its town, and the Spa sits by the railroad. Cinder Springs is a short walk off the Ember Way, which is right, but it has no building to say who keeps it.

### What Cinder Springs should do (Claude D; no new rules, no rewards)

1. **Move `springs_bath` onto the tier-5 shelf,** beside the pools, for example at (2240, 5480) along `springs_walk`. Put the rail and towels against the shelf's back face.
2. **Grow the pools** to at least 64 x 40 and 48 x 32 (two adults sitting), stepped down the shelf. The upper pool spills into the lower one over a lip, so the declared `outlet` reads.
3. **Give the springs a keeper:** a small bathhouse or lean-to with a door onto the shelf, as Stardew's changing rooms are. Or a hut for Cinderwick's charcoal burners, who use it.
4. **Remove the 24 px tier-5 strip** at x 2152 to 2176 below the shelf, or make it the shelf's drain down to the sea, with a fall.

Per AGENTS.md, a bath stays decorative, with no energy or other effect.
