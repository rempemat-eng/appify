After `--fresh --zone=crossroads --stage=homecoming --demo --quit` the save holds `quests: {}`, `village.restored: {}`, `spawn: "start"`, only `visited:crossroads`. Either the stage is not written, or it is written before the stage applies, so a reload drops the forge-restored state.
