## Findings, most severe first

### 1. Dust Gulch stands a stone's throw from flowing lava

- **Where:** Dust Gulch, west (0, 0), its east side: the mine head and the water tower at about x 230 to 300, y 90 to 290. The Scorched Pass, west (1, 0): the lava channel's west bank lies at x 36 to 46 for y 0 to 192, and at x 55 to 106 for y 192 to 576.
- **Owners:** Claude D (`sunstone_zones.py`, the Scorched Pass), and Claude A for the plan that moves the fire country.

In the browser, from beside the Gulch's water tower, the glowing channel fills the right third of the screen. Between them:

- The sand meets grey ash in a ragged band about 30 px wide, with a few scrub tufts.
- The ash runs straight down to the lava, with no crust band apart from the glow.
- The town's buildings are 60 to 140 px from molten rock.

This breaks three rules:

- world-rules 9.3: a settlement is at least a screen (300 px) from hostile ground. Matt, 20 Oct: the gulch "should be entirely in the desert and the lava somewhere else".
- world-rules 9.2: sand goes to scrub, then ash, then crust, then lava, and each step is at least 96 px across.
- S1: "Sand never meets molten lava: scrub and cooled crust come between."

The 15:53 review passed this seam on tone alone. It should not have.

The world plan draft (#96, v5.3) puts Hearthold 75 s down the Canyon Road from Dust Gulch, so the real fix comes with the continent canvas.

**Action until then (Claude D, decorative only):**

- Move the Scorched Pass's lava channel and its crusted basin into the zone's east half (x 200 and beyond). The bridge stays on the path's line.
- Lay cooled crust next to the lava, then ash, then dusted scrub, then sand. Each band should be 64 to 96 px, with lobed edges.
- If that cannot be done before the canvas, Claude A should list the contact in `OVERRIDES` with Matt's quote and the plan's date, so it is a known exception and not a pass.

### 3. The Woodland's waterfall has no face to fall over

- **Where:** the Woodland, valley (-1, 0). The stream from the Hollow's escarpment runs along level ground at x 52 to 75, y 20 to 245. It then drops as a white column from a pile of mossy boulders at x 60 to 115, y 245 to 300, into a pool at y 290 to 305 on the same level of grass.
- **Owner:** Claude E (`valley_zones.woodland`).

The water is visible above the fall and below it on one tier. A fall therefore only works if there is a step there, and none is drawn. This breaks:

- world-rules 7.2: water changes tier only at a fall, straight down a face;
- world-rules 3.5: height changes only at a way between levels.

(The 19 Oct notes call this the Woodland's "spring", but the stream feeding it is drawn.)

**Action:** either

- run a low mossy rock step (16 or 32) across the Woodland at about y 250, ending in a slope at each side, with the stream falling over it and the path climbing it at a stair; or
- drop the white column and let the stream run through the boulders as a riffle into the pool.

Also from the same view:

- The path stops about 30 px south-east of the oak's door, at x 250, y 290 against a door at x 215, y 235.

## Comparison: the Woodland against Stardew Valley's Cindersap Forest and Link's Awakening's Mysterious Forest

Earlier runs compared the valley coast, the isles, Neon, Cogsworth, the Frostline, the town, Driftwood and Hearthold, and Claude D compared Sunstone. No review has yet compared the Woodland. I have not played these games for this note. Claims stay at what the sources say.

### Cindersap Forest (Stardew Valley)

From the official [Stardew Valley wiki's Cindersap Forest page](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Forest):

- The forest is a set of named homes, each in its own corner:
  - Marnie's Ranch in the north-east;
  - Leah's Cottage "south of the path to Pelican Town";
  - the Wizard's Tower on the western side;
  - the Abandoned House south-west of the pond.
- The water has a source and a shape: the river "extends the river from Pelican Town". There is a large pond in the north-west, and the waterfalls are by the Abandoned House.
- One landmark stands at the forest's heart: the Big Tree, "between the lake and Marnie's Ranch".
- The forest opens on four sides: the Farm, Pelican Town, the Sewers and the Secret Woods. The way into the Secret Woods is closed by a drawn object, a "Large Log blocking the entrance".

### The Mysterious Forest (Link's Awakening)

From [Zelda Universe's walkthrough](https://zeldauniverse.net/guides/links-awakening/walkthrough/mysterious-forest/) (a long-running fan site):

- You find your way by landmarks, one after another: a cave covered by "a hollowed-out log", boulders round a second cave, a raccoon by a tree near the entrance, and the witch's hut past "spooky-looking trees".
- Every block on the way is a drawn thing: bushes, boulders, stones, the raccoon.

### What reads better there

Both forests are places with people in them and a sequence of landmarks, not a lawn with trees on it. Their water explains itself: a river that comes from somewhere, and falls where the ground drops. Their closed edges are objects.

NapAttack's Woodland has good parts:

- the hollow oak with a door;
- the stone circle;
- the stream, its pool and the footbridge.

But:

- The oak's door has no one behind it: no path to it, no doorstep, no sign of a household.
- The fall has no step (finding 3).
- The trees are mixed one by one, evenly, across the whole zone: round broadleaves, birches and firs side by side. world-rules 9.7 asks for clumps of 3 to 7.

### What NapAttack should change (Claude E with Claude B's props; decorative only)

1. **Make the oak a home.** Bring the path to its door. Give it one personal object that names who lives there, for example a lantern on a peg, a woodpile or a tiny mailbox. The resident can stay unseen.
2. **Make the water read.** Draw the step that the fall drops over (finding 3), so the pool and the fall become the Woodland's landmark, seen from the garden's west edge.
3. **Clump the trees by kind** (9.7), using the forest kit (#155):
   - firs together towards the Hollow's escarpment;
   - broadleaves round the stone circle;
   - birches in one bright group by the stream.
4. **Add one small pocket** behind a fallen trunk from the border blockers (#159), on the Woodland's west side towards the old forest. Inside, a view or a resident, never a keepsake (world-rules 6.10).
