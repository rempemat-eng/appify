# The map's secrets (PROPOSALS)

Matt, 4 Nov 09:42: "continue to improve the map. really think about ... where little easter eggs and tads bits could be on the maps".

These are small things drawn on the continent overview (v56, `docs/vision/research/blockout/map_secrets56.py`) that reward looking, the way Zelda's, Pokémon's and Eastward's maps do. Each one could later become a place to find in the game.

**Every row is a PROPOSAL.** Nothing here is adopted until Matt and the story lane (K) take it up. No quest, item, reward or gameplay data exists for any of them (world-plan rule 10: decorative finds give no rewards, costs or chores until a quest is written for them).

**How they are drawn.**
- No secret has a label. A few sit beside a place that already has a name.
- Most read only at 3840; a few are just visible at 1920.
- Two sit where the weather thins: the north cloud bank opens a little over number 5, and the east fog thins over number 11.
- `map56-3840-secrets.png` (made with the v56 renders) circles and numbers each one, matching this table.

They tie to the game's story (game-design v2, section 8.1):
- the night Lumen cracked and the starfall;
- the old folk who built the stone circles to hold the moon's light;
- the Company, which buys shards and sells their light.

The eight spots world-plan section 14 reserved are all kept, as numbers 4, 12, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21 and 24. Their positions now follow the v55 warp.
