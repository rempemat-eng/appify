## How to read it

| Status      | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **kit**     | A density-3 piece is in `game/kit/pieces` with purpose, role, relations and collision, measured against Eastward at Bramble's height. Every kit piece is still a `candidate` until the library gate and Matt approve it. |
| **rework**  | An older picture exists; the ledger entry is cited (`prop/<name>`: grade, fix). A ledger KEEP was graded at the web density, so it still needs a density-3 kit drawing before a rebuilt region may use it.               |
| **missing** | Nothing exists. The row gives its purpose, placement rule (in the kit's metadata terms), size class and material.                                                                                                        |

Placement uses the kit's metadata (`placement.requires` / `forbids` read by `scripts/art/kit/relations.py`; `boundary` and `enclosure` by `enclosures.py`): `near route 2` means a route within 2 cells, `at_corner route` a junction, `within house 3` a home within 3 cells. Every prop also forbids `on route` and `on water` unless it says otherwise. Size classes are the density-3 table in [size-guide.md](size-guide.md) (`scripts/review/sizes.json`), in art px (A = 74).

## Why the Brackens set was built next (this PR)

Development-plan Phase 6 puts **the Brackens and the Frostline** first after the Crossroads/woodland slice. The Brackens go first of the pair: their named things are almost all props (drove well, sheepfolds, shepherd's hut, broken fold, stile and bench, cairns), they reuse the farmyard's animals and enclosure rules, and the moor's ground (heath, peat hags, the drove road) is small enough to add with them (round 2); the Frostline still waits on terrain decisions the Y19 report leaves open (glacier, tarn headwall, snow shelves). The Drover's Road also carries the story slots the whole world uses most (cart with a broken wheel 57, cold fire ring 55, cairn 54, marker stone 63), and the moor's own materials (gritstone, heather, peat) that no kit had. The junctions' fingerposts, the washing line and the wayside shrine landed in the village commons (#430, `commons_*`) while this was built, so the set reuses them. Built in `scripts/art/kit/sets/brackens.py`; study in `sets/brackens_study.py`.

## Gaps this PR leaves

- **The moor's dressing is a post-pass, not yet a bake hook.** `heath`, `peat` and `drove` are terrain pieces with fills (additive: no existing material, manifest entry or bake hash moved), and the drove road bakes as a dirt route. The heather clumps, hag faces, trods and outcrops are painted by `sets/moor.dress` over the bake, with the pieces' shadows recovered by comparing bakes with and without them. A zone built on the moor needs `bake.py` to call that painter; that edit moves every bake hash, so it belongs to whoever next regenerates the baked zones.
- **The stile runs along x only**; a stile along y comes when a plan needs one. Every other wall cell (15 join masks) and both gate axes exist and are checked in the study's wall lab (`brackens_study.wall_lab`).
- **`scripts/review/sizes.json` matches names by substring**, and "cairn" there is the character Cairn (class `large`, 83 to 95 tall), so the stone cairn `brackens_cairn` can be taken for him. The piece keeps its name; tightening the match is for Codex.
- **Metadata the kit cannot say yet** (as #430 found for the woodpile): the hermit's fire "at the end of a side trod" is approximated as `near route 6`; the bench's "a view" is not expressible.
