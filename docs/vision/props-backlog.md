## How to read it

| Status      | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **kit**     | A density-3 piece is in `game/kit/pieces` with purpose, role, relations and collision, measured against Eastward at Bramble's height. Every kit piece is still a `candidate` until the library gate and Matt approve it. |
| **rework**  | An older picture exists; the ledger entry is cited (`prop/<name>`: grade, fix). A ledger KEEP was graded at the web density, so it still needs a density-3 kit drawing before a rebuilt region may use it.               |
| **missing** | Nothing exists. The row gives its purpose, placement rule (in the kit's metadata terms), size class and material.                                                                                                        |

Placement uses the kit's metadata (`placement.requires` / `forbids` read by `scripts/art/kit/relations.py`; `boundary` and `enclosure` by `enclosures.py`): `near route 2` means a route within 2 cells, `at_corner route` a junction, `within house 3` a home within 3 cells. Every prop also forbids `on route` and `on water` unless it says otherwise. Size classes are the density-3 table in [size-guide.md](size-guide.md) (`scripts/review/sizes.json`), in art px (A = 74).

## Why the Brackens set was built next (this PR)

Development-plan Phase 6 puts **the Brackens and the Frostline** first after the Crossroads/woodland slice. The Brackens go first of the pair: their named things are almost all props (drove well, sheepfolds, shepherd's hut, broken fold, stile and bench, cairns), they reuse the farmyard's animals and enclosure rules, and their ground needs nothing the kit lacks; the Frostline still waits on terrain decisions the Y19 report leaves open (glacier, tarn headwall, snow shelves). The Drover's Road also carries the story slots the whole world uses most (cart with a broken wheel 57, cold fire ring 55, cairn 54, marker stone 63), and the placement audit's open gap, "a signpost for junctions". Built in `scripts/art/kit/sets/brackens.py`; study in `sets/brackens_study.py`.

## Gaps this PR leaves

- **Terrain for the moor** (heath, peat cuts, damp flushes; gritstone bluffs) is a terrain-kit task, not a prop: the study stands on lawn, path and worn.
- **One gate axis.** The drystone gate and stile run along x only; a gate along y (`gate_ns`) and the T and end cells (`esw`, `e`) come when a plan needs them.
- **Signpost arms.** `placement.points` (#430's round 3) is not set on the fingerposts: `rules.points` counts a road two cells wide as a way out north and south of itself, so a T junction on the Drover's Road reads as four ways. An arm pointing north would also hide behind the post in the three-quarter view, so the arms point east (two places) and west; a north arm needs a drawn convention first.
- **Metadata the kit cannot say yet** (as #430 found for the woodpile): the hermit's fire "at the end of a side trod" is approximated as `near route 6`; the bench's "a view" is not expressible.
