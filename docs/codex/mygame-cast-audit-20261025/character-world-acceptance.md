# Character and world acceptance

Review target: `rempemat-eng/appify@2f88ca5381388affc1e5f457678cf09a9e481749` (25 October 2026). These are acceptance criteria for Claude A's new cast and the C60 combat port, not claims that the behaviours have been rendered or play-tested on that revision. Claude A owns the cast, actors, world and integration; C60 remains with the economy slot. The recovered numeric contract in draft #245 governs combat where the older #241 studio differs.

## Shared requirements

- Keep the actor's ground footprint, support plane/elevation, airborne lift and image pivot distinct. Draw the body, worn equipment and attached effects from the same transform. Ground shadows and depth sorting follow ground/support, not the top of a jumping sprite.
- Use each actual S/N/E/W drawing's measured anchors. West retains anatomy and upper-left lighting. Keep continuous simulation aim separate from cardinal body facing. A muzzle's image Y is not planar reach or a complete height calculation.
- Compare faces, grips, connected equipment, foot contact and action poses at actual 2x FHD play size against both light and dark terrain. Metadata counts do not establish visual quality. Cairn's current larger target is intentional; do not shrink him to the human target.
- Release once per action instance at canonical simulation time, with the contact pose displayed before effects sample its anchors. Frame count, slower studio cadence and extra recovery drawings do not change damage, cooldowns or form duration.
- Frontal guards must use aim consistently and retain the held pose. Guard break, hit, defeat, interruption and powered expiry need explicit state transitions. A form's helmet, mask, rifle or casing persists through every applicable movement and reaction.
- Use the world owner's collision and support contracts for walls, door clearances, ledges, stairs and decks. Movement abilities must not disable collision. Check the movement sweep and destination support, including a target just beyond a wall or deck edge.
- Tether, projectile, blast and linked-field interactions with walls and line of sight need an explicit owner decision. The recovered open arena does not establish a wall policy. Never infer cross-wall or cross-deck reach from the drawn effect.
- Costumes and elemental identities grant no swimming, heat immunity, climbing, traction or terrain immunity. Unimplemented traversal access stays unavailable.
- Pause and inactive/focus-loss handling must freeze the shared clocks and clear stale held/buffered input. Scene teardown, defeat and authorised switching must cancel pending releases and clear or transfer owned temporary entities under one declared policy. No old turret, tether or projectile may act in an unloaded scene.
- Use the canonical-unit-to-world conversion agreed by Claude A and C60 once. Art density alone is not permission to change combat reach, speed, field radius or turret spacing.
