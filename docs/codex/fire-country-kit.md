## What already exists and which source wins

The existing systems are the W0 construction families, the editor's candidate piece library and physical checks, the region plan/kit painters, and the `mygame.town_plan/1` data. This document extends their requirements, not their implementation. The source register below pins each input to a full commit, path and Git blob.

- **S1/S2:** proposed v55 Hearthold and Cinderwick at PR303 `4984438d`. These are the current town composition inputs, not approved shipping layouts. Hearthold has32 records/28 counted plus4 separate landmarks; Cinderwick13 records/13 counted.
- **S3/S4:** separately delivered, unmerged PR335 and PR334 town additions. Hearthold becomes35 records/31 counted plus the same4 landmarks; Cinderwick17 records/15 counted, including two uncounted net sheds. Treat these as review deltas, never as already present in S1/S2. Their direct JSON changes still need generator adoption and material recomputation.
- **S5/S6:** the older region brief and region-plan explanation supply volcanic geography, work sites, approaches and unique assemblies. Their v5.3 coordinates,32px tier assumptions, earlier town sizes and ramp counts do not override v55 town geometry or later ground/edge rules. No old coordinate is copied into a new placement here.
- **S7:** current v55 README counting, frontage and theme rules. Detached streets stay detached. A row's fronts and its counted contribution are different; landmarks and net sheds are not extra counted homes.
- **S8/S9/S10:** ground rules, Eastward edge geometry and size guide. World-pixel footprint, cell envelope, art extent, collision, apron and elevation are separate measurements. One cell is8 world px; density3 uses24 art px/cell and a74-art-px adult. S9's older density2 heading is historical; S10's density3 section and W0's current sizing basis are used for art routing, with no rescaling of world coordinates.
- **S11/S12:** world/cast and progression context. The circle/ring device is decided; weapon numbers, many story applications and service costs remain proposals/placeholders. A kit requirement grants no combat effect, reward, respec, tier purchase or new interaction.
- **S13/S14:** W0 catalogue and Y14 format precedent. W0 entries are concepts, not approved live pieces. Y14 demonstrates explicit provenance/deferred fields, but its old Highgarth forms, dimensions and seventeen-building coverage are not fire-country inputs.

## Review order and explicit questions

These ranks order **requirements decisions**, not a new production schedule or additional family allocation.

1. **P0 — physical contracts before drawing:** settle elevation/stair/retaining interfaces and material pairs for basalt/ash/lava/shore; source-authored flow and crossing continuity; measured door/approach anchors. W0's zero/unallocated fields and proposed selectors cannot pass as runtime geometry. Resolve W01/W14 black-sand overlap and W07 basalt-sett reuse without duplicate surfaces.
2. **P1 — town identity and ordinary repeatable parts:** decide older-made domestic/shop/workshop/boathouse/shed construction against S1–S4, then castle landmark/wall/gate assemblies. Can W08/W09 supply joints without carrying valley/coastal/industrial appearance? Which exact facings, wall returns, roof widths and yard variants are needed? The source form ranges bound proposals; neither all intermediate widths nor new variants are demanded.
3. **P1 — source inconsistencies:** b004 barracks versus “guildhall”; five `forge`-form trades versus actual fire hosts; Cinderwick's harbour-office buyer without building ID; Cinder Springs/Ember-forge generic ground prose versus basalt/ash material counts and missing landmark structures. Ask source owners; do not silently repair towns here.
4. **P2 — custom sites and existing hooks:** confirm actual bridge stages, great-forge footprint/platform, bathing place, dragon/crater/cave assemblies and named waystone/Moss/Company anchors. S11/S12 connect Ashe/Ember and forge services as narrative context; they allocate no dialogue, quest, cost, reward, craft effect or additional NPC routine.
5. **P2 — clearance guidance conflict:** S10 records a legacy300world-px settlement-to-hostile-ground guide,64px green-to-lava rule and a fire-country band description (crust4–8/ash16–32). W0 has proposal cell clearances and S1 describes a town between flows. These are different scopes/ages, not an approved combined lava setback. Reviewer must select the applicable current contract and measure the eventual layout; this supplement invents no distance or declaration of compliance.
6. **Later promotion gate:** no JSON intake, schema edit, material ID, painter call, collision/foot/overhang assignment or buildable/placeable piece is produced. After W0 semantic questions and concept review, a separately claimed follow-up can author schema-valid records through the existing owner/promotion process. Drawing, raster gates/Y12 sheets, Eastward comparison, placed-zone checks and Matt/Claude PR OK remain separate.
