## Physical rules and interactions

One logical cell is 24 texels = 8 world px; review walk cells are 12 texels = 4 world px. Source geometry collision is in world px in raw definitions; built-index collision/deck consumers use their documented texel representation. Door rectangles always use cells. Never infer units from magnitude. Relation verdict parity and full physical validity are different oracles.
