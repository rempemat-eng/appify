## How to use the classifications

- **Reuse lead:** existing source art, metadata or a documented family worth reviewing first. It is a lead for a future authorised lab/integration, not permission to place or ship it.
- **Metadata rework:** an explicit TODO, unresolved binding or recorded source discrepancy. An omitted optional field is not automatically a schema failure or art defect.
- **Legacy art rework:** an exact historical ledger REWORK record, with its stored fix. Similar names do not prove that a current Godot sprite has those pixels or faults.
- **Missing binding/art:** the requested family has no explicit PNG or receiving-library binding in this pinned source. This says nothing about unpublished scratch work. A concept envelope is not measured sprite geometry or collision.
