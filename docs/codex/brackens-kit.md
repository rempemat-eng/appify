## Placement and scale contracts to carry forward

S05 builds height → water → materials → routes → structures → role props → clutter. Water stays on one tier until a real drop, with a source and valid outlet; falls match their channel, rim and landing pool. No route/worn material goes under water except a bridge span. Routes connect real destinations, use straight cell-aligned runs, constant width, rounded bends (0.4–12 cells) at least two cells apart; route feet stay clear. Doors open onto routes; footprints do not overlap. Trees keep cell-aligned trunks and uncut crowns; rocks sit with contact shadows and require an explicit stacked state to overlap. Clutter stays out of water and route centres. These are existing checks, not proof that a candidate satisfies them.

S07 distinguishes standing stones from their whole ring, low plants from tree crowns, and a hut body from its door and reserved apron. W0's `size_px` fields below are nominal proposal fields, not measured solids or permission to reinterpret units. Some families omit size/foot/support information completely. Preserve class-specific density-3 sizing and request final feet/solid footprint/overhang/interaction anchors rather than deriving them from a rectangle. C12's 4×2-cell landing is reserved access, not a solid collision rectangle; it is not a new shepherd-house footprint.

S13’s pine seed records `size_px=[125,240]`, `size_cells=[5,10]`, `foot=[62,240]` and a collision rectangle `[-5,-5,10,5]`. Its own audit calls collision and shadow first guesses and leaves role relations/composition/clearance outstanding. These are source fields, not an approved Brackens footprint or a conversion between the two size representations.

The regional threshold prose includes a long mixing band and 160px/4s ambience cross-fade, while its benchmark prose separately says 3s. Keep those older figures as provenance questions: no new fixed band width or audio timing is set here. S04's no-ramp valley route wording and W0 cliff apertures likewise do not author a new connection on Brackens terrain.
