# MyGame settlement building-use source audit

28 October 2026 · `slot=loom` · `X3-use-audit/r1` · [claim 6476078178](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/issues/149#issuecomment-6476078178)

## Decision brief

The existing settlement-life checker cannot yet distinguish a home from an occupied workplace. PR #330 and its source-pin successor #332 require one provisional representative whose `home` is each building with a non-empty `occupants` field. Every one of the **380 building records in the 21 current PR #303 town files** has that field populated. This is a count of source records, not population, households, frontages or the README’s counted-building total.

The pinned text explicitly supports residential use for **272 records** and mixed shop/residential use for **27 records**. For **81 records**, residential use of that exact building remains unresolved. No record explicitly establishes that residential use is excluded, so this audit assigns **zero records to explicit work-only**. A workshop, office or barn label establishes a function; it does not by itself establish where its workers live.

The practical blocking case is small: `canalside-b003` and `canalside-b009` are `the workshop`, with `the master and apprentices`. Their source does not state that those people live there. The checker rejects an otherwise equivalent eight-representative Canalside diagnostic that omits those two buildings, but accepts a ten-representative diagnostic that assigns them as homes. In contrast, `fernhollow-b002` and `fernhollow-b005` explicitly say `the shopkeeper, living above`. Those records support mixed use, while the checker still requires every `workplace` to be null and deferred.

**Decision for Matt:** should future X3 coverage use explicitly authored residential/mixed-use building IDs, with workplace bindings authored separately, and leave the unresolved records unassigned until their use is supplied? For the unresolved records, specify any living quarters and their exact building IDs; this audit does not turn a work role into a home or allocate people between buildings.

The labels below describe evidence only. They add no schema, authoring rule, character, household size, job, home assignment, workplace binding or schedule. The existing Gullwick packet remains a bounded residential planning packet; these findings do not reopen its passed source-reference work.

## Contract and minimal diagnostic examples

The relevant expressions are unchanged between the two pinned checkers:

```python
occupied = {b["id"] for b in source["buildings"] if b.get("occupants")}
homes = [r.get("home") for r in residents]
require(set(homes) == occupied and len(homes) == len(occupied),
        "coverage: one provisional representative per occupied home")
```

Both also require `workplace is None` and `workplace_status == "deferred"`; the day destination is the building’s `faces` street, and the other provisional phases use the building ID. These checks validate the existing packet boundary, not a general residential/workplace model.

Other unresolved examples require care:

- `fernhollow-b007` and `hearthold-b003` say `the priest, living beside it`. This locates accommodation beside the chapel; it supplies no separate home building ID and does not say the chapel itself is the home.
- Inns name `the innkeeper, staff and guests`. This is work and accommodation evidence, but it does not identify which listed people have their permanent home at the inn.
- Keeps name `the lord, the household and the guard`; mills name `the miller and family`. These associations do not explicitly identify living quarters within the recorded building.
- Neon Harbour tenements say `several, one to a floor`. The existing one-representative-per-building check is not a headcount or a household-per-floor model.
- In current #303, `highgarth-b001`, `highgarth-b002` and `highgarth-b020` have temple/pagoda/bathhouse forms but generic chapel/tower/school purposes. This audit preserves that source conflict. Pending #331 is a separate exporter correction and is not silently substituted.
