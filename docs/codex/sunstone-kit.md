## Authority, units and existing systems

No map generator or historical terrain was regenerated for this audit.

G3's density-3 section supersedes older density-2 art figures: an adult is 74 art px, an 8-world-px logical cell is 24 art px. Town `rect_px` and `footprint_cells` describe world layout; G4 opaque-picture size classes, W0 authoring envelopes, reserved clearance and final collision are separate measurements. Every W0 number in the family table is a PROPOSAL, never a measured sprite or approved placement. Runtime cell/elevation bindings remain with W1 and the editor owners; this document makes no conversion or rounding rule.

The existing E1 system already has materials/transitions, derived faces, width-specific features, candidate prop scenes, JSON/scene round-trip, walk/relations checks, the dock and a gated library. W0 inventories 129 concepts and identifies shared versus custom construction. Current main includes Rivet's Y9 pause correction and the isolated engineering integrations; this report does not rebuild any of them. Source302 remains unapproved. Library gating, exported runtime semantics and actual composed tests precede placement.

## Town coverage and source boundaries

T1 has 20 building records and `buildings_counted=20` (target14–22). T2 has 38 building records, of which two depots have `counts_as=0`, and `buildings_counted=36` (target30–45). These are read from proposed data, not new quotas. T3's overview still says38 Dust Gulch buildings; preserve the distinction between front/record count and counted buildings. The complete 58-record appendix below retains identity and source dimensions without approving them.

Palm Spring has Spring Row, the caravanserai, Channel Row, Palm Row and the date gardens. Its stated identity is water, wind and caravans, off the rail. Its first view is the caravanserai gate then the pool and palms; its quiet corner is the pool steps. Gardens follow two channels, with houses on dry gravel above them. T1's ground is packed sand/stone round the pool, adobe yards and irrigated green only along the channels. Date palms, figs and vegetables are named source species, not permission to add other species. Mirra is the oasis diviner in S1. No water sale, maintenance, timer, earning or player task is introduced.

Dust Gulch has Front Street, the Depot, Water Street, West Row, the corrals, goods yard and ore yard. It is the desert line's railhead and the Lode's ore destination, with steam and Pendle; T2 restricts shard lamps to the depot. Packed-sand streets and boardwalks meet sand/adobe yards; irrigated gardens belong along the Amber. The water tower, chapel, saloon, ore bins and river gardens are named landmarks. T2 includes both a building district and an edge-use district with id `dust-gulch-d-the-corrals`; this audit preserves both and assigns no new district ID or interpretation. District prose names sheriff/assay functions that are not necessarily a unique building record. Do not infer their building, job or resident bindings.

Both plans name later shortcuts (Mirra's garden path; the stationmaster's dry-wash gate and the post-flood Amber bridge). They are source context, not authorization to wire triggers, timings, gates, residents or routes. T1/T2 resident/routine lists remain empty; Y16 does not fill them. S1's stopped-spring/drill story and S2's weapon/service proposals add no mechanics to this report.
