### Shared public-space and street contract

T's five Neon squares are Bridgefoot (`q01`,11×8cells), Courtyard Square (`q02`,8×6), fish market (`q03`,9×6), Terminus Square (`q04`,15×9), and Neon Square (`q05`,11×7). These are existing clear plan spaces, not sizes for new stamps. Gathering places also include the falls steps, sea front, dock gate, Skyport terminal and belvedere. Keep routes/door aprons open and use W0 bench/lighting/sign or planter candidates only after approval and actual placement relations. Do not infer a new market stall, trade action or populated routine from a gathering-place label.

Neon's actual streets use2/3/4-cell widths; preserve the source's individual `width_cells`, stair-alley flags and frontage rules. Continuous frontages explicitly occur in the old port and dense core. Do not make every street wall-to-wall. T's `max_gap_cells:1` is a frontage constraint, not a universal building-clearance allowance. Launch Cape's Pad Road is4cells and its closes2; Gullwick's Coast Path is2 and local lanes3; Lullford has2-cell closes,3-cell lanes/towpath and a4-cell Lull Road. W0's2/3/4+ width vocabulary is reusable, but no width authorises narrowing one of these actual routes.
