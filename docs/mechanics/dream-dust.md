# Dream Dust: every source and sink, and what to change

Claude L (gameplay mechanics), 21 Oct, checked against `main` at 5df4f4b. **The map is fact; section 6 is proposals for Matt, each with its sim evidence.** Pacing (how fast unlocks come) belongs to Claude K's progression spine; this page hands K the ledger and the levers. The saved field is still `grass`.

**Demo clock.** One real second is one "simulated minute": `tick()` runs every 1000 ms and a nap pays `elapsed` whole seconds (`lib/game.ts`), and the UI says so ("Demo time: 1 real second = 1 simulated MIN. +1 Dream Dust per MIN.", `components/game-menus.tsx:128`). Time with the app closed counts. A nap left running for a real 8 hours pays 28,800.

## 3. What C33's sim models, and what it misses or models differently

Files: `scripts/economy-inputs.ts`, `scripts/economy-model.ts`, report `docs/codex/economy-sim.md`. It is a solo ledger with 3 scenarios (TODAY = demo clock, L2, G10) × 3 players over 8 weeks.

**Modelled, and matching the code:** hatch 20 (S1). Nap at 1 per credited minute, with TODAY at 60 credited min per real min, which is the demo clock (S2). Missions 20/60/150, Mountain gated at L2, Mountain hat or +50 (S3 to S5). No nap pay during outings, and no pay after the deadline (matches the freeze at 1251). Early return forfeits the reward. Spar 5 × first 3 wins a day (S6). Feed 5/+20 mood with mood decay of 1 per 3 s awake (K1). Levels 50 × level (K2). Hats 30/40/60/150 (K3). Spend visits fit the 180 s play budget. Card grants are counted (no Dust value).

**Missing (the sim says so in "Limits"):**

- S7 raid wins, S8 the +20 defence award, K4 −10 attack loss, K5 defender loss: all multiplayer, excluded.
- S10 Together bonus: excluded.
- **S9/K6 "SIMULATE INCOMING RAID" button.** Not mentioned anywhere. It is a live local faucet and drain: +20 or −30% (min 10) at most once a real minute while exposed, with no play-budget check.
- **Ways** (`lib/world-ways.ts`, 3,005 for W1 to W9 plus 200 for S1, S2, S4; S3 unpriced): excluded as proposals. Also excluded are #143 outfits and comforts, #146 home base, and L2 medicine and L3 repairs.

**Modelled differently from the code:**

- **Mountain hat.** The sim grants the _first unowned_ hat in list order (CAP 30 first); the code picks at random (`lib/game.ts:1284`). The code can hand out the CROWN (150) first, which makes buying all hats cheaper and earlier than the sim shows. The sim acknowledges this.
- **Shopping policy.** At most 1 level and 1 hat per visit, and 1 feed attempt per visit. The code has no such limit beyond the play budget and affordability. So "days to level N" in TODAY and L2 is limited by visits, not by Dust.
- **Nap length.** The sim pays only scheduled, explicit rests. The code pays any open nap for as long as it runs, with no cap, so a forgotten nap pays every real second.
- **Spar wins** are assumed outcomes credited at the visit. In the code they need an actual battle within the play budget.
- **L2 and G10 are not code.** Rest 2× / Focus 1.5× (L2) and the G10 240/day taper at 25% plus the 60 night gift are scenario rules only. The well-kept gift is set to 0 in both (no value agreed). The sim applies the window multipliers pro rata to outing rewards (its own interpretation). Under G10 a qualifying night (≥240 min, starting in Rest 23:00 to 07:00) replaces per-minute pay with 60, capped at 1 a wake day.
- The rhythm windows (Rest 23 to 07 daily, Focus 9 to 17 weekdays) are sim inputs. `lib/daily-rhythm.ts` is planning-only and pays nothing (`lib/daily-rhythm.ts:1`).

### For Claude K

- The ledger above is the whole economy today: income today is naps (over 94 % in every TODAY row of C33's sim), missions and three spar wins a day; spending is levels (50 x level), four hats (280) and feeding (5).
- Ring levels proposed for the unlock table (docs/mechanics/fight-content.md, `rivals`): Dave 1 (the tutor), the hut warden 3, the tinkerer 4, the cowhand 5, the smith 7, Dave at the Neon court 8, the station botanist 10, the stargazer 12; rings and rivals follow Claude K's spine (#197), and K's pacing puts their arrival at days 2 to 37 for a regular rester and 2 to 56 for a light one.
