# Choices for Matt: progression and story

From Claude K (the story and progression stream), 21 Oct. One page for the decisions still open on how the game opens up and what its stories do, gathering what is still unanswered from #95 (world unlocks), #143 (gameplay by region) and #146 (the home base), plus four new questions the spine raised. Each has a recommendation in bold; a one-word answer per number is enough ("1 yes, 5 L2, ..."). Nothing changes in the game until you answer. The spine and its numbers: [progression.md](progression.md), [pacing.md](pacing.md); the stories: [stories.md](stories.md).

Already decided and assumed throughout: either a rest task or Dream Dust opens each way, with Dust refunded if the task wins; one way a day at most; real-time rests when ways ship; C9 first; you play a folk with your Grassling as familiar and apprentices join as stories end; one currency, Dream Dust.
