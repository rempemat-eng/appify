## How a source gap prevents a start

A normal quest starts only when its exact event reaches `Quests.notify` and every start condition holds; `zone_built:<id>` consults the actual zone loader. Definitions, dialogue entries and matching words in a plan do not create those events. [game/core/quests.gd:145–191](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/blob/ba710f78544e894c72fa6fce807b8d5d5f88813b/game/core/quests.gd#L145-L191); [game/core/quests.gd:218–231](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/blob/ba710f78544e894c72fa6fce807b8d5d5f88813b/game/core/quests.gd#L218-L231)

The shipping registry contains Woodland, Crossroads, Undercroft and five lab/test zones; it omits Frostline, ice caves, story Brackens, the barrow, Driftwood and Halt. Frostline has a scene, but is not registered. Debug-only `--try-zone` temporarily adds a scene for one run; that is not ordinary shipping access. [game/data/zones.json:2–9](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/blob/ba710f78544e894c72fa6fce807b8d5d5f88813b/game/data/zones.json#L2-L9); [game/core/zones.gd:18–37](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/blob/ba710f78544e894c72fa6fce807b8d5d5f88813b/game/core/zones.gd#L18-L37); [game/core/zones.gd:55–65](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/blob/ba710f78544e894c72fa6fce807b8d5d5f88813b/game/core/zones.gd#L55-L65)

Dialogue loads independently of scene placement, and its `talk:<id>` notification occurs when that talk is actually invoked. Missing speakers below mean absent ordinary scene bindings, not absent dialogue prose. [game/core/dialogue.gd:21–28](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/blob/ba710f78544e894c72fa6fce807b8d5d5f88813b/game/core/dialogue.gd#L21-L28); [game/core/dialogue.gd:54–68](https://github.com/rempemat-eng/appify/blob/ba710f78544e894c72fa6fce807b8d5d5f88813b/game/core/dialogue.gd#L54-L68)

### The two requested restore gaps
