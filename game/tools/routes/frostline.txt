#! args: --try-zone=frostline --zone=frostline --spawn=woodland
#! save: frostline.save.json
# The Frostline (game/kit/zones/frostline.json), played with real input. It is a lab until Matt approves
# its pieces and nothing in the world leads to it yet, so the route opens it by name (--try-zone,
# core/zones.gd) from a save made at the top of the Col Path: the slice and the torn roof done, Moss's
# loaf carried past the shard crows at the ridge col (the climb is what arriving here finishes).
# The loaf to the warden, the ring and the ibex on the High Fells end to end, the ice-cutter by the
# tarn, Under the snout waiting for the ice caves, and down the Col Path through the door.
# Commands: tools/play_driver.gd.

step in at the top of the Col Path: the climb is done, the warden waits for her loaf
expect zone frostline
expect quest a1_bread_for_the_bothy active warden
shot 01_arrival

step up the trod to the Col hut's door: the loaf
goto 330 840 10
expect prompt Talk
talk HutWarden
expect said Bread! Real bread, from Moss!
expect quest a1_bread_for_the_bothy done
expect quest a1_the_ring_is_shut active fells
shot 02_warden

step up the switchback to the tarn: the ice-cutter at her sledge
goto 552 540 10
goto 744 552 10
goto 744 312 10
goto 444 312 10
expect prompt Talk
talk IceCutter
expect said I cut ice for the inn at Cogsworth
shot 03_tarn

step over the snow to the High Fells: free the ibex
goto 744 300 10
goto 744 204 10
shot 04_fells
fight 180
expect flag camp_cleared:fells_ibex
expect quest a1_the_ring_is_shut active report

step back down to the warden: the ring opens, the caves stay sealed
goto 744 312 10
goto 744 552 10
goto 552 552 10
goto 330 840 10
talk HutWarden
expect said The fells are clear?
expect quest a1_the_ring_is_shut done
expect flag ring_open:frostline
expect quest a1_under_the_snout none
talk HutWarden
expect said sealed with new ice
shot 05_ring_open

step down the Col Path and out through the door
goto 552 1032 10
door 552 1090 woodland
expect zone woodland
end
