#! save: first_night.save.json
# The first night lost, then held (Matt, 13 Nov: "Shard dims until held"; core/raids.gd, world/raid_night.gd),
# played with real input from the save first_night.txt starts from. The player takes both hurdles off the
# pads and stands on the garden walk while the shardstruck walk down an open road and break the shard
# lantern. The lantern is dark from that moment: on the square, after a quit and a load, after walking out
# and back in, on the prepare screen and through the next night, and nothing else is taken (the Glint, the
# restores and the quests are as they were). The second night is put back as Hale set it and held from the
# square, and the lantern is lit again and stays lit; Hale says it was the one night.
# No pilot plays the loss: it is `button`, `wait` and `waitfor` only. The second night uses `defend` as
# first_night.txt does, with the state checked before it at the moments the beats depend on.
# Commands: tools/play_driver.gd.

expect dimmed no

door 951 1356 crossroads
