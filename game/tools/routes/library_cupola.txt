#! args: --zone=crossroads
# The library's bell cupola framed whole near the library (Matt, 13 Nov: "bell tower optional, cupola
# drawn"). The camera follows Bramble 8 world px above the feet, so from the square the cupola's top stood
# above the picture; in front of the library the camera now eases up until the cupola fits, with Bramble
# kept inside the safe insets (world/camera_framing.gd, data/camera_frames.json), eases back on the way
# out, and cuts under reduced motion. Commands: tools/play_driver.gd.

step in the square, the plain follow: the cupola stands above the picture
wait 1.0
expect zone crossroads
waitfor 30 banner none
goto 744 900 20
wait 1.0
expect framed Props/If_cupola_empty/CupolaEmpty no
expect inview 48
shot 01_square

step in front of the library: the cupola whole on the picture, Bramble on it too
goto 720 660 16
wait 1.0
expect framed Props/If_cupola_empty/CupolaEmpty yes 4
expect inview 48
st
shot 02_library

step back in the square: the plain follow again
goto 744 900 20
wait 1.0
expect framed Props/If_cupola_empty/CupolaEmpty no
expect inview 48

step under reduced motion it cuts in, with no ease to wait for
key Escape
expect paused yes
button Reduced motion
expect setting reduced_motion true
key Escape
expect paused no
goto 720 660 16
wait 0.3
expect framed Props/If_cupola_empty/CupolaEmpty yes 4
expect inview 48
goto 744 900 20
wait 0.3
expect framed Props/If_cupola_empty/CupolaEmpty no
expect inview 48
